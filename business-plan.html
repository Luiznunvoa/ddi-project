01

Executive Summary

FylFox is a cloud-based SaaS platform that helps UK small businesses organise, check and visualise supplier-related financial documents. Supplier invoices, purchase orders and credit notes are uploaded into a single searchable workspace, where AI-assisted processing classifies each document and extracts its key fields. Configurable checks then compare the extracted data against related documents and business rules, flagging duplicates, missing information and mismatches for a person to review.

FylFox does not replace the accounting package, provide outsourced bookkeeping, or make final payment decisions. It occupies the checking step between receiving a document and approving it for payment — the step that is currently done by eye, when there is time.

02

Problem & Opportunity

Supplier documents arrive through scattered channels — email attachments, supplier portals, printed post — and are stored across shared folders and spreadsheets with no reliable link between related records. Three consequences follow:

  • Hunting for documents. Finding one invoice means searching several inboxes and folders, every time a supplier queries a payment.
  • Duplicate payments. The same invoice is chased, re-sent, re-entered — and paid twice before anyone notices.
  • Mismatches found late. Ordered 100, delivered 90, invoiced 100. Spotted at month-end, if at all.

The competing alternatives each leave the gap open: a shared folder makes documents findable but performs no checks; an accounting package records transactions but is not designed for reviewing the source documents behind them; outsourced bookkeeping removes the work but also the visibility and control.

Product & Initial Scope

The MVP covers one workflow: checking supplier invoices against purchase orders and credit notes. English-language documents, GBP amounts, UK date conventions.

In scope for the first pilotDeliberately deferred
  • Upload of PDF, JPG and PNG documents
  • AI-assisted field extraction with a human review step
  • Catalogue searchable by supplier, type, date, amount, reference
  • Invoice ↔ PO ↔ credit-note matching with flags for duplicates, gaps and mismatches
  • Exceptions queue with plain-language explanations and recorded outcomes
  • Dashboard: documents processed, awaiting review, recent exceptions
  • Single workspace with role-appropriate access
  • Accounting-system integrations
  • Delivery notes and supplier statements
  • Customer-specific configurable checks
  • Multi-entity workspaces
  • Non-UK document formats and currencies

A narrow scope keeps the pilot fast enough to produce a clear answer about the core workflow.

Go-to-Market & Validation Plan

Validation runs in stages. Each answers one question, and each is capable of stopping the project. The problem and the workflow are tested before the MVP is built.

StageQuestionMethodEvidence needed to continue
1. Customer discoveryDoes the problem exist and recur?Interview 8–12 UK finance/admin staff plus owners and bookkeepers; walk through their most recent invoice task. Do not pitch.A consistent majority describe the same friction unprompted, with concrete recent examples
2. Workflow and demandIs this the painful part, and worth paying for?Map receipt → approval; rank the most painful tasks; react to a realistic subscription price.Checking ranks among the top pains; interviewees accept a plausible price and agree to trial
3. Prototype testingCan users complete the workflow?Clickable prototype with 5–8 target users.Most complete the tasks unaided and interpret the flags correctly
4. MVP pilotDoes it work on real documents, and do users stay?4–6 weeks with 3–5 businesses; compare FylFox output against existing manual checks.Measured against thresholds set before the pilot begins
5. DecisionExpand, refine, or stop?Review results against those thresholds.Workflow completed, extraction reliable, alerts useful, credible willingness to pay

Acquisition channels to test

  • Accountant and bookkeeping partnerships — highest leverage; one partner introduces many clients.
  • Accounting-software marketplaces — listing and integration give distribution plus credibility.
  • Direct outreach — slowest and most expensive, but the fastest source of discovery evidence in Year 1.

Operations, Risk & Compliance

Main operating costs

Cloud hosting and document storage, AI extraction and processing, product development, customer support, security and compliance.

Riskiest assumptions, in order

#AssumptionMitigation
1Extraction and matching are accurate enough on real documents that reviewing the output is faster than doing the work manuallyHuman-in-the-loop review on every uncertain field; measure corrections per document during the pilot and set a pass threshold in advance
2Alerts are specific enough to be trusted — a noisy queue will be ignoredTrack useful vs. unhelpful alerts as a headline pilot metric; tune rules for precision over recall
3The problem justifies another subscription and another system to learnFree trial and pilot pricing lower the commitment; behavioural evidence (continued use) governs the decision, not stated enthusiasm
4Document-volume pricing covers per-document cost at small scaleInstrument cost per document from day one; overage pricing and plan limits protect the margin

Compliance

Supplier documents contain personal and commercially sensitive data, so UK GDPR obligations apply from the first pilot: a lawful basis and data-processing agreement with each customer, UK or EU data residency, encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, retention limits aligned to the plan tiers, and a documented sub-processor list covering the AI provider. Customers retain their accounting records; FylFox is a processor, not a system of record.
