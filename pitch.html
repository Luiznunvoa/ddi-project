How FylFox works

Three steps. No new habits to learn.

1

Drop the document in

PDF, JPG or PNG. Invoices, purchase orders, credit notes — however they arrived.

2

FylFox reads and files it

AI-assisted extraction captures supplier, date, amount and reference, then catalogues it. Uncertain fields are shown for a quick human check.

3

You review the exceptions

Matched documents pass quietly. Duplicates, gaps and mismatches go to a queue with a plain-English explanation and a next step.
